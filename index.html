Missouri Environmental Incident Response From Improper Disposal

Between 1993-2019

Project Overview

This map shows the amount of environmental incidents related to improper disposal in Missouri from 1993 to 2019. The counties are on a color-coded scale based on the number of incidents reported. Also shown are locations of the larger populated cities in the state.
